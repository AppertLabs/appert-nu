1. Subscription and free trial

The Streamert phone app needs a subscription: €9.99 a year or €1.99 a month, including VAT. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts and you are charged through your Apple or Google account. The Streamert TV app is free.

2. Renewal and cancelling

Your subscription renews automatically for the same period at the price then in force, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You cancel in your account settings in the App Store or Google Play. After cancelling, you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. We will tell you before any price change takes effect.

3. Payment, withdrawal and refunds

Apple or Google handles the payment, VAT and refunds under its own terms. Ask for a refund through Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play. If an automatic yearly renewal wasn't what you intended, email support@appert.nu and we will help you get a refund for the remaining full months. Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected.

4. Stations

Your subscription pays for the app and its features. It does not pay for the radio stations. Stations are third-party streams, found through the free Radio Browser directory (radio-browser.info) or added by you as your own link. We don't own, host or control these streams, and we can't promise a station is available, works properly or has particular content. Stations keep the rights to their streams, names and logos. Only add links you are allowed to use.

5. Use

You may use Streamert for personal, non-commercial listening. Don't record, redistribute or resell streams through the app.

6. Liability

We build Streamert carefully, but we provide it as it is. As far as the law allows, we are not liable for indirect damage or for the content and availability of third-party streams. We may update these terms. We will announce important changes in the app before they apply. Dutch law applies, but you keep the mandatory protection of the law of the country where you live. Questions or complaints: support@appert.nu.
