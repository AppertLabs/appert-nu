What Streamert does

Listening. Nothing else.

No podcasts and no endless catalogue. A list of good stations and a player that does its job.

Over 1,800 stations

A curated list of commercial-free internet radio stations. Search, filter by genre and keep your favourites. Swipe to move to the next station.

What’s playing

Title and artist of the current song, with cover art when it can be found. Got Apple Music? Look the song up there with one tap.

In the background

Keep listening with your phone locked or while using another app, and control the radio from your lock screen. On iPhone you can also send the sound over AirPlay.

Apple TV

A dedicated Apple TV app. On the same Wi-Fi, iPhone and Apple TV stay in sync: same station, start and stop together.

Sonos

Play on Sonos speakers on your own Wi-Fi, in one room or several at once. The speaker keeps playing, even if you close Streamert.

No account, no tracking

No ads, no analytics and no account needed. More in the privacy policy.
