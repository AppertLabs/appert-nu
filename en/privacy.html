Privacy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics for us (on Android, Google does get usage data from the photo route, see below) and no account.
  • We have no server of our own: data from the apps does not reach us.
  • To work, Fietsert sends your location or search text, plus your IP address, to map, search, routing and weather services run by others (see below).
  • Your routes stay on your phone and, if you have it switched on, in your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google backup (Android).
  • If you email us, we keep your email for up to 1 year after your question is closed.

Who is responsible

Fietsert, Streamert and this website (appert.nu) are by Joost Wellen (AppertLabs), the controller under the GDPR. Contact: support@appert.nu.

We show no ads, use no analytics or tracking software, except Google ML Kit's built-in usage statistics on Android, build no profile of you and don't sell or share data with advertisers. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

Fietsert: what stays on your device

  • Location – for the map, nearby junctions, guidance and break spots during a ride. Only with your permission; on iPhone in the background only while you navigate a route. Location is optional: without it you load a province yourself under Manage regions, and everything works except centring the map on your position, "Use my current location", guidance that follows your position and break spots.
  • Routes and settings – on your device. On iPhone and Apple Watch, if iCloud is on, also in your own iCloud with Apple; we have no access to it. On Android, your own Google backup may include your routes if you have it switched on; we have no access to that either.
  • Apple Health (iPhone) – only if you choose to save after a ride are the workout, distance, energy burned and route you rode saved in Health. The app reads nothing from Health. To withdraw consent: choose "Don't save", or turn off Fietsert's access in the Health app or in Settings.
  • Apple Watch – uses your location for the map and navigation. The workout session while navigating keeps the app on screen and is not saved in Health.
  • Photo route – junction numbers are read from the photo on your device (iPhone: Apple Vision; Android: Google ML Kit). The photo and the text go nowhere. On Android, ML Kit does send usage and performance data to Google (device model, Android and app version, an installation ID, speed and error codes, plus your IP address), not the photo or the text. ML Kit terms.
  • Sharing a route and usage counters – you share through your phone's share menu yourself; the counters stay on your device.

Fietsert: services the app uses

Each service receives your IP address and technical details of the request, and is responsible for what it does with them (see their privacy policy).

  • Apple Maps and Apple WeatherKit (iPhone, Watch) – the map area, the start and end point of the route to the network, and the location where you plan a loop (for the wind). For the EEA, Apple Distribution International Ltd. (Ireland) is the controller. Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation – the map on Android (tile.openstreetmap.org) and search (Nominatim) on all platforms. They receive the area you view and your search text, so please don't type personal details there. OSMF privacy policy.
  • FOSSGIS e.V. (Germany) – junctions and break spots via Overpass (your position with a radius of about 20 km, or only the province name when downloading), and on Android the route to the network via OSRM (start and end point). FOSSGIS privacy policy.
  • iCloud – syncing routes, settings and navigation state between your own devices, only if iCloud is on.

Purchases: Fietsert is free and currently has no in-app purchases. If we add them, they go through Apple or Google; we then receive no payment details, only sales reports without your name (such as order number, date, country and, for Google Play, possibly city and postcode), kept for 7 years for tax purposes. We will update this policy beforehand.

Streamert

No account, ads, analytics or tracking, and no access to location, microphone, camera or contacts. Your settings are stored on your device. Your own stations are stored on your device and, on iPhone, in your own iCloud with Apple, so they appear on your Apple TV. The app connects to the station you pick (stream and title), to music databases for cover art (such as iTunes, Deezer, MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive or the station's own service, by artist and title, without an account) and over your own Wi-Fi to your Apple TV and Sonos speakers. They receive your IP address. Apple Music opens only when you tap its button.

  • Radio Browser (public station directory, radio-browser.info) – when you search: your search text. When you add a station: its stream link and name, to look up its country. Only if you turn on ‘More for you’ (off by default): a genre and country of your own stations. No click or vote data.
  • Station websites – when you find a station via its website or share a page from Safari, your phone loads that page and the stream and logo links on it. Logos in search results are loaded from the logo link listed in Radio Browser, usually the station's site.
  • Station logos – to show a station's logo, Streamert fetches it from Radio Browser or the station's own website; these services see your IP address. The logo is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud (iPhone) or Android backup.

This website

Hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., US), with Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., US) in front for DNS and traffic. Cloudflare receives, on our behalf (as a processor), your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser and any automatic error reports (Network Error Logging). GitHub may log IP addresses for security. The site itself uses no cookies, analytics, tracking, or external fonts or scripts.

If you email us

We use your email address, name, message and attachments only to answer your question and fix the problem. Feel free to remove personal details from screenshots. Our email is hosted by TransIP B.V. (the Netherlands). Your email is read by AppertLabs, with help from an AI assistant (Grok Bot by SpaceXAI, United States); a person stays responsible for every reply. We keep your email and attachments, including the copies in our own mailbox, for up to 1 year after your question is closed. Working copies are deleted as soon as it's closed.

Legal bases (GDPR Art. 6 and 9)

  • Location and search text to the services above, and iCloud sync – necessary to provide the feature you ask for: on iPhone and Watch under the licence agreement (Apple's standard EULA) (Art. 6(1)(b)). On Android, where there are no terms yet, our legitimate interest in providing that feature (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Workouts in Apple Health – health data, only with your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a)).
  • Sales reports (only with paid features) – legal tax obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Streamert: searching, adding stations and loading websites/logos – needed for the feature you use (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f)). ‘More for you’ – only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); turn it off in Settings to withdraw it.
  • Photo route on Android: ML Kit usage data to Google – based on your consent (the notice before your first photo route; you can withdraw it in Settings) (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Support email and the website – our legitimate interest in answering your question and keeping the site secure and available (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long

On your device, in iCloud and in Health: until you delete it. Support email: up to 1 year after closing. Sales reports: 7 years. At the services above: according to their own policies (the OpenStreetMap Foundation says it keeps server logs only temporarily).

Data outside the EU

  • Inside the EEA: FOSSGIS (Germany), Deezer (France), TransIP (the Netherlands).
  • United Kingdom: OpenStreetMap Foundation, under an adequacy decision (valid until 27 December 2031). Map tiles may be served from outside the EEA.
  • US: GitHub and Cloudflare (and Google LLC, for Google Play purchases and ML Kit on Android) are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Apple uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for transfers to Apple Inc. Support email via SpaceXAI (Grok Bot) is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can ask for a copy at support@appert.nu.
  • Streamert: music databases, station directory and radio stations: MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive (United States), radio stations and their websites worldwide, and Radio Browser (location unknown) may be outside the EEA. No adequacy decision or other transfer safeguard applies to these connections.

Children

The apps ask for no account, name, age or email address and are not specifically aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights and complaints

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and you can withdraw any consent at any time. Because we receive no data from the apps, there is usually nothing for us to show or delete; you manage data on your device, in iCloud and in Health yourself, and for data held by the services above, contact those parties. Email requests to support@appert.nu; we reply within one month. Unhappy? Please tell us first. You always have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If the apps start handling data differently, we update this policy and the date at the top. The Dutch and English versions are the same.
