Privacy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

In short
  • No ads in the app.
  • No tracking, no analytics and no third-party ad networks.
  • Your data stays on your device and, for Fietsert if you choose, in your own iCloud with Apple.
  • No account needed.

Who we are

Fietsert (cycling app for iPhone, coming soon to Android) and Streamert (internet radio for iPhone, Apple TV and Android) are apps by AppertLabs. This privacy policy covers both apps and this website (appert.nu).

No tracking and no ads

Our apps show no advertising and contain no third-party analytics or tracking software. We don’t build a profile of you and we don’t sell or share data with advertisers.

Fietsert: what data the app uses

  • Location – on your iPhone, to plan your route and show the next junction. Only with your permission.
  • Routes and settings – stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, synced through your own iCloud account with Apple. We have no access to it.
  • Apple Health – a ride is only saved as a workout if you allow it.
  • Photos for a photo route – only the photo you choose or take, to turn it into a route.
  • Apple Watch – the Watch app uses your location for the map and navigation, and while navigating it starts a cycling workout session so the app stays on screen; that session is discarded when you stop. Routes reach your Watch through your own iCloud.
  • Purchases – Fietsert is free and has no in-app purchases.

For maps, search and sync the app uses Apple services, which are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Streamert

Streamert has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. To work, the app connects to:

  • Radio stations – the stream and the title of the current song come directly from the server of the station you pick.
  • Cover art – to find a cover, the app looks up the artist and title of the playing song in music databases such as iTunes (Apple), Deezer, MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive. No account or login cookie is used for this.
  • Your own Wi-Fi network – to keep your iPhone and Apple TV in sync and to find and control Sonos speakers. This traffic stays on your local network.
  • Apple Music – only when you tap the Apple Music button yourself does the app open the song as a search in Apple Music.

Your settings and favourites are stored on your device. Streamert does not ask for access to your location, microphone, camera or contacts.

This website

Your rights

Because we don’t collect personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to show or delete. You manage data in iCloud or Apple Health yourself in your iPhone settings. Questions? Feel free to get in touch.
